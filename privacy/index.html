Privacy

When enabled, Google Analytics 4 measures visits to this website, including the pages people read and the approximate cities visitors connect from. Google derives approximate location from IP addresses. City estimates can be inaccurate.

Google Analytics receives information such as page URLs, referral sources, browser and device details, and interactions with the site. It uses first-party cookies, including _ga and _ga_<container-id>, to distinguish browsers and sessions. These cookies have a default lifetime of two years, subject to browser limits and Google settings. This site’s Analytics code disables Google signals and advertising personalization.

Google processes this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on for supported browsers.
